About
Preview clothes on an R15-style avatar and prepare templates for shirt or pants. All processing is done locally.
Templates
When you drop a texture, this app generates Roblox-ready template layers for the selected type (Shirt/Pants).
Your clothing files are never uploaded. This is a pure client-side app running on Favnc.
